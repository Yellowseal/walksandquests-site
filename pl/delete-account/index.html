Delete Your Account

Barcelona Walks & Quests — developed by Stanislav Kotyk

This page explains how to delete your Barcelona Walks & Quests account and all the data associated with it. There are two ways: from inside the app, or by writing to us.

The short version. Open the app, go to your profile, and tap Delete Account. Your account and your entire progress history are removed from our servers immediately, and the copy saved on your device is cleared. It cannot be undone.

Delete it from inside the app

  1. Open Barcelona Walks & Quests.
  2. Open your profile (tap the profile icon).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. A confirmation appears. Tap the confirm button to proceed.

Deletion happens right away and cannot be reversed. When it finishes, the app returns you to the sign-in screen as a fresh, empty user.

If you can't open the app

If you have lost access to the app but linked an email address to your account, write to privacy@walksandquests.com from that same email address and ask us to delete your account. We will complete the deletion and confirm, and in any case respond within one month.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes, immediately and permanently:

  • Your account — the anonymous identifier created automatically the first time you opened the app.
  • Your email address, if you chose to link one.
  • Your entire progress history — the list of stops you checked in at, and the badges derived from it — stored on our servers.
  • The progress saved locally on your device.

We keep no backup copy and there is no recovery window: once deleted, the data is gone.

What is not affected

Any purchases (individual districts or the whole-city bundle) are held by the app store — Apple or Google — against your store account, not by us. Deleting your Barcelona Walks & Quests account does not cancel or refund them; you can restore them at any time with “Restore purchases”.

If you simply uninstall the app without deleting your account, and you never linked an email, an anonymous record with no personal data may remain on our servers. Write to us at the address above and we will remove it.

The app collects no other personal data, so there is nothing else to retain — see our Privacy Policy for the complete picture.
